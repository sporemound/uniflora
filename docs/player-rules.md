# Player rules

The Missing Interior is a public, asynchronous Discord game about shared observation,
maintenance, translation, ecological limits, and reconstruction. Positions 0-6 are complete and
can be played entirely through deterministic slash commands.

## Core rules

- Play in the configured public game channel. The game sends no clues by DM and requires no
  private coordination, voice participation, hidden inventory, or secret player knowledge.
- There are no permanent player classes and no speed challenges. Contribute when you can; the
  public record remains available through `/interior recall`.
- Position 0 opens with one first-come public event: a participant names the rootglass seed and
  chooses whether to plant, open, or keep it. The incompatible choice changes no puzzle answer,
  but its physical mark and authorship remain in public state through later positions.
- Use only confirmed public entities, observations, records, samples, and conditions. An
  unsupported assertion cannot create evidence or unlock progress.
- Important proposals require confirmation from someone other than the author. Positions 0-5
  require at least three distinct participants and contribution functions; final reconstruction
  requires at least four of each.
- Before a proposal is filed, observation, coordination, and calculation are interleavable
  Preparation activities. Filing a proposal opens an exclusive Response window; ordinary actions
  resume only after that window resolves.
- Each participant gets one primary contribution per cycle. Once the Position's contributor
  minimum is met, someone already counted may assemble the proposal without needing a second
  primary action. An administrator is not required to advance or close an ordinary cycle.
- One person cannot satisfy the collective requirements by repeating commands. Equivalent
  actions are deduplicated and cannot farm counters or contribution credit.
- A failed action does not erase the position. It returns public guidance and, where relevant,
  records a useful limit, evidence deficit, overload, or failure for later work.
- Proposals, stack reactions, kickers, and triggers refer only to public state. A response stack
  has at most four entries, one per participant, resolves last-in-first-out, and cannot contain a
  nested stack.
- Natural-language interpretation is optional and supports only a narrower action set. Explicit
  slash commands are the authoritative, complete path and do not require OpenAI.

Use `/interior next` when you want one useful contribution without reviewing the history,
`/interior position` for a compact current briefing, `/interior recall` for the full confirmed
public state, and `/interior accessibility` for explicit alternatives. The complete syntax is in
the [command sheet](command-sheet.md).

## Whole-settlement events

In Position 1, meaningful accepted circulation actions can cause an unexpected public event.
The roll is stable: retrying the same Discord interaction cannot reroll it. If chance produces no
event, one is guaranteed after four to six eligible actions.

An event remains open until its displayed clock deadline; other players' actions do not shorten
that window. Its message and `/interior recall` provide three context-specific response buttons;
their names change with the threatened place while their strain, displaced-burden, and
capacity tradeoffs remain explicit. One Mycotroph can
resolve it there or with `/interior intervene`; no proposal, observation, group threshold, or
confirmation is required.
The same participant cannot resolve two consecutive events. Every method executes successfully,
but `brace` adds strain, `divert` displaces burden, and `release` loses temporary capacity while
lowering escalation. Three unmitigated events restore Position 1 to its recorded beginning.
Earlier completed positions and inherited scars remain. Settlement updates never use mass or
role notifications.

## Provision Works rules

The later arc distinguishes production from provision. Useful goods and stable livelihoods may
count as public benefit, but benefit never cancels water draw, waste, heat, risk, labor,
maintenance, land loss, extraction, or who controls the system.

Remediation follows this order:

1. refuse unnecessary production;
2. reduce harmful inputs;
3. redesign production;
4. reuse only verified-safe material;
5. separate clean and contaminated flows;
6. contain unavoidable waste;
7. remediate compatible contamination;
8. monitor long-term effects; and
9. manage spent substrate.

Treatment capacity does not create permission to pollute. Visible clarity is not proof of safety,
returned water is not automatically usable, and spent substrate is not automatically compost.
Unknown or incompatible material, low fungal viability, excessive flow, saturation, or missing
containment prevents successful treatment.

Position 6 accepts multiple reconstruction models, but each must reduce unnecessary throughput,
protect public water, preserve livelihoods or provide a transition, separate flows, put source
reduction before remediation, contain spent substrate, fund monitoring and maintenance, preserve
history, establish public or worker-community accountability, and define reassessment and
shutdown authority. Once confirmed, terminal reconstruction remains at Position 6 and is
read-only.
